ABOUT MY WORK

Prediction is a starting point.
Evidence needs examination.

I examine the reliability of model outputs, the quality of supporting evidence, and the limits of forensic interpretation. My work combines direct investigation, structured datasets, machine learning experiments, and human review.

I am pursuing a PhD in Digital and Cyber Forensics under Professor Qingzhong Liu. I joined SHSU in August 2025 and expect to graduate in July 2029.

01 / RESEARCH

Questions I investigate

From recovered artifacts to reviewable findings.

01

IoT & network forensics

Investigator support, network security, and cross-dataset reliability of encrypted traffic classification.

External evaluation identified a 4.35% false positive rate on 947,997 benign flows using CatBoost.

Python · CatBoost · SHAP · Flow analysis

02

Android & AI reporting

Direct examination of Android evidence and evaluation of reports generated through three LLM APIs.

Six reports and 612 claims evaluated for evidence support, citation validity, and fact recall.

ALEAPP · Python · Gemini · Llama · Qwen

03

Windows memory forensics

Process-level dataset construction and reliability-oriented evaluation of rule-derived classification.

45 dumps, 1,899 process records, and 18 features. Labels represent documented rules.

Volatility · GroupKFold · SHAP

04

Quantum security

Simulation-based QKD attack classification and forensic interpretation of operational log patterns.

Two synthetic datasets, each with 5 million records. Cross-generator tests examine changed conditions.

QF-LOG · Simulation · Random Forest · XGBoost
